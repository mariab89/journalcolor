# JournalColor — Gratitude Journals & Cozy Coloring Books for Mindfulness

JournalColor is an independent creative brand focused on gratitude journals, mindfulness tools, and cozy coloring books designed to support emotional well-being, creativity, and self-reflection.

## What is JournalColor?

JournalColor is a creative project dedicated to designing intentional journaling and coloring experiences that encourage calm, gratitude, and personal growth.
The brand blends mindfulness practices with warm, cozy aesthetics to create meaningful daily rituals.

## What We Create

JournalColor designs:
- Gratitude journals for daily reflection and positivity
- Mindfulness and self-care journals
- Cozy and relaxing coloring books
- Printable and physical journaling content
- Creative tools for emotional wellness and self-expression

## Why JournalColor?

JournalColor focuses on simplicity, emotional clarity, and intentional design.
Each journal and coloring concept is created to help people slow down, reflect, and reconnect with themselves through writing and creativity.

## Project Status

JournalColor is currently in development.
New journal concepts and coloring book collections are being designed and prepared for future release.
